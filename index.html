How these numbers were worked out
  • "Fee earned" is the Commission column in the August 2026 payroll report (5% or 8% of the courier's basic + incentive).
  • FT and PT follow the Position Type column. FT-M, BSC and Bulky count as FT; PT-M counts as PT.
  • Many PT couriers work only a few days, so a typical PT earns us much less than the average. Half of PT couriers brought in RM15 or less.
  • New joiners bring in less because they only worked part of their first month. From the second month they earn closer to the "all couriers" figure.
